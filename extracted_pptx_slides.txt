=== SLIDE 2 ===
The Problem: AI Tutors Are Unmonitored & Unreliable
AI tutors now serve millions of students globally — yet there is zero pedagogical oversight on the content they generate. The consequences are serious and growing.
🚀 Explosive AI Adoption
AI tutors serve millions of students globally, yet response quality remains entirely unchecked and unmonitored.
🧠 Hallucinations Are Rampant
LLMs confidently generate incorrect facts, misleading students during critical learning phases with no warning.
⚠️ Dangerous Dependency
Students copy AI answers without understanding — directly undermining conceptual learning and critical thinking.
🌐 Language Barrier
Most AI tutors operate only in English, excluding 1.5 billion+ regional-language learners worldwide.
🔍 No Evaluation Layer
There is currently zero pedagogical oversight or quality scoring on AI-generated tutoring content.
✅ EvalTutor Fills the Gap
A real-time evaluation platform that monitors, scores, and continuously improves AI tutoring quality.

=== SLIDE 3 ===
Objectives
EvalTutor is designed around five core objectives that together create a comprehensive, trustworthy AI tutoring ecosystem.
Monitor AI Quality
Score every AI response for pedagogical accuracy, clarity, and educational value on a 0–100 scale.
Detect Hallucinations
Flag responses with high hallucination risk before students act on potentially incorrect information.
Measure Dependency
Track over-reliance on AI answers versus independent reasoning to promote genuine learning.
Multilingual Support
Enable seamless tutoring in regional languages via automatic language detection and translation.
Analytics Dashboard
Provide students and educators with real-time learning insights, progress metrics, and weak topic identification.

=== SLIDE 4 ===
How EvalTutor Works: End-to-End Workflow
A seamless 7-step pipeline transforms a student's question — in any language — into an evaluated, scored, and translated AI tutoring response delivered in real time.
Student Input
Student submits question in any language
Language Detect
Auto-detects input language (langdetect)
Translate to English
Translate source to English (googletrans)
AI Tutoring
Groq Llama-3.3-70B generates response
Each step is automated and executes in milliseconds — the student experiences a seamless, real-time tutoring interaction while the evaluation engine works silently in the background to score every response.

Working Methodology: 10-Step Process
From question submission to dashboard update — EvalTutor's complete processing pipeline executes automatically in under two seconds per interaction.
01
Student Submits Question
Student types a question in any language via the chat interface.
02
Language Identification
Langdetect identifies the input language automatically.
03
Query Translation
Googletrans translates the query to English for AI processing.
04
AI Response Generation
Groq Llama-3.3 generates a structured, pedagogically sound AI response.
05
Response Translation Back
Response is translated back to the student's native language.
06
Evaluation Engine Analysis
Evaluation Engine analyzes response quality across multiple dimensions.
07
Quality Score Calculated
Quality Score is computed on a 0–100 scale using a multi-dimensional rubric.
08
Hallucination Risk Flagged
Hallucination Risk is classified as Low, Medium, or High per response.
09
Dependency Level Logged
Dependency Level is measured and recorded for the session.
10
Dashboard Updated
All metrics are pushed to the student dashboard in real time via Chart.js.
